# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
CS (Company Secretary) candidates in India preparing for the rigorous ICSI Executive and Professional exams. They are studying under pressure, need strict pointwise feedback on their answers, and require authentic, exam-pattern practice.

## Product Purpose
Provide an AI-powered evaluation engine that grades handwritten law and tax answers line-by-line against strict ICSI standards, explains exactly why marks were lost, and generates tailored question papers.

## Brand Personality
Cinematic, academic-elite, authoritative, minimalist, and focused.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS marketing pages with bright pastels, emoji-laden features, or cartoon illustrations.
- Overwhelmed, text-heavy landing pages that look like standard web-coded templates.
- Jittery/jumpy panel-swapping scroll animations.

## Design Principles
1. **Cinematic Drama & Focus**: Use deep, dark forest tones (#0A231C) with a singular glowing lime-yellow accent (#E8F29E) to establish high-end, premium focus.
2. **Editorial Typography**: Large Sora headings paired with Playfair Display italic serifs to evoke academic prestige and examiner authority.
3. **Fluid Morphing**: Section details should transition seamlessly in place, showing progressive changes rather than jarring visual jumps.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast ratio (WCAG AA) between the dark background (#0A231C) and text (#FFFFFF, #E8F29E).
- Clear, focusable inputs and keyboard navigation support.
